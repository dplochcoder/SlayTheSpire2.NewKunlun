Starting relic:
  Jade System: At start of combat, gain 1 Qi Charge. Shuffle one Talisman Dash into your deck.
  Orobas: At start of combat, gain 3 Qi Charges.

Internal damage: Decrease by 1 at start of turn. If HP is lost, convert all internal damage into hp loss, then set to 0.
Qi Charge: Max 3, spent up to card max for bonus effects on play.
Talisman: Debuff which disappears after Detonate or 2 turns.
Parry: When an attack would be fully blocked, instead of losing block, lost 1 Parry and gain 1 Qi Charge.
Imperfect: If attacked, gain {Amount} Internal Damage.
