# New Kunlun

New Kunlun is a Slay the Spire 2 content mod based on the game [Nine Sols](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1809540/Nine_Sols/). It includes a new [character](#Yi) along with a fully fleshed out set of Act replacements including new Ancients, Events, Monsters, Elites, and Bosses. Collect the 9 [Sol Seals](#the-sol-seals) to unlock a brutal fourth Act and confront Eigong.

You can play the base game Acts with [Yi](#Yi), or explore New Kunlun with your own favorite character, the mod contents are designed to be interchangeable balance-wise with the base game. To get the full experience, it is recommended you constrain act selection to only New Kunlun acts, as there are numerous Ancient, Event, and Quest interactions which span multiple acts.

## Yi

[Yi](https://ninesols.wiki.gg/wiki/Yi) is the main protagonist of Nine Sols. His card pool revolves around several core pillars:

-   [Parry](#parrying-and-qi-charges) attacks to gain [Qi Charges](#parrying-and-qi-charges).
-   Spend [Qi Charges](#parrying-and-qi-charges) to deal damage.
-   Trade [Internal Damage](#internal-damage) for acceleration.
-   Arm the Azure Bow for true power.

### Parrying and Qi Charges

Like the Regent, Yi's card pool heavily interacts with a secondary resource, _Qi Charges_. Unlike the Regent, Qi Charges are not a _required_ resource for playing cards, but an _optional_ one that triggers additional benefits only if they are available.

Yi starts with a maximum capacity of 3 Qi Charges, and gains them by _parrying_ attacks. Whenever an incoming attack is full-blocked, it is considered a _parry_, and Yi gains a Qi Charge for it.  Many cards in Yi's kit interact with Parries and Qi Charges.

#### Talisman Dash

The Talisman Dash is Yi's primary method of spending Qi Charges. It allows Yi to _mark_ an enemy, and then next turn, _detonate_ the mark to deal massive damage that scales with the Qi Charges spent. Timing of the dash is critical in order to maximize efficiency of Qi Charge spending, and avoiding painful passes on the Detonate next turn.

### Internal Damage

Yi's primary debuff is Internal Damage, which can be inflicted on enemies as well as self-inflicted. Internal Damage is effectively _delayed_ damage, that isn't yet resolved against the creature's current HP.

Whenever a creature takes direct damage, all Internal Damage on the creature immediately resolves into real damage. Many cards in Yi's tool kit revolve around taking Internal Damage for extra benefits, or drawing power from Internal Damage on the enemy. Ordering is crucial for maximizing the utility of the enemy's Internal Damage and managing the risk of Yi's own Internal Damage.

### Azure Bow

The Azure Bow is an extremely powerful weapon in Yi's arsenal with high damage and utility, but with setup cost. It is similar to the Regent's Sovereign Blade, in that specific cards are required to bring it online and that fast deck-cycling makes it more powerful.

The Azure Bow, once spawned, stays in your hand forever unless removed through other means. Each shot requires an Azure Sand Magazine, which can be spent on one of three powerful arrows:

-   Cloud Piercer: Severely weakens and damages a single target.
-   Shadow Hunter: Powerful multi-hit damage for a single target.
-   Thunder Buster: Massive AOE damage spread over time.

## Sol Seals

Sol Seals are unique relics obtained from each of New Kunlun's bosses instead of the standard rare card reward. To 'unlock' the seal, you must successfully finish a run in which you obtain it. It is not possible to obtain all 9 Sol Seals in a single run.

-   Seal of Benevolence: Defeat Lady Ethereal (Act 2)
-   Seal of Discipline: Defeat Jiequan (Act 2)
-   Seal of Equality: Defeat Goumang (Act 1)
-   Seal of Innovation: Receive from Abacus (Act 1)
-   Seal of Insight: Defeat Ji (Act 3)
-   Seal of Integrity: Find Fuxi's Soul Sanctum (Act 3)
-   Seal of Legacy: Defeat the Sky Rending Claw (Act 1)
-   Seal of Loyalty: Defeat Yingzhao (Act 1)
-   Seal of Selflessness: Defeat Nuwa & Fuxi (Act 3)

Once you have unlocked all 9 Sol Seals, you will be given the option to proceed to Act 4, the Research Institute, after Act 3. Fight through the halls of mutant experiments to confront Eigong.
